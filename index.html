About Dawood Bakers

A neighbourhood grocer, grown into a full store.

Our Story

From one bakery counter to your whole grocery list

Dawood Bakers started as a small bakery counter, known for the smell of fresh bread that greeted the whole street every morning. Customers kept asking for one more thing — a bag of atta, a bottle of oil, eggs for the week — until the bakery counter grew into a full grocery store.

Today we stock staples, spices, cleaning supplies and baby care, alongside the bread Dawood Bakers has been known for. What hasn't changed is the promise we opened with: fair prices, honest weight, and a shelf that's always stocked.
